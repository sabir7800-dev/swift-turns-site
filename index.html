Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

What's included in a standard turnover?

A full reset between guests — beds stripped and remade, kitchen and bathroom cleaned and sanitised, floors done, surfaces wiped down, and rubbish removed, finished with a walkthrough check before we leave.

Do you bring your own cleaning supplies?

Yes — our team brings its own eco-friendly, plant-based cleaning products and equipment as standard, so you don't need to stock anything. Let us know if you'd prefer fragrance-free products and we'll happily use those instead.

What happens if the guest arrives before you're finished?

We build a buffer into every turnover slot and will message you straight away if we're running behind schedule, so you're never caught by surprise.

How does payment work?

Payment is taken before your scheduled visit.

What areas do you cover?

We currently cover all of North London, North West London, and parts of West and East London. Get in touch with your postcode and we'll confirm.

Can I cancel or reschedule a booking?

Yes — we ask for at least 2-3 hours' notice before your scheduled visit, though the earlier you can let us know, the better we can accommodate you.
